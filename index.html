Co-op horror for 2 to 8 players on an offshore oil rig at night. Keep the rig running until the dawn helicopter. Something down there has learned your voices.

Five nights. Nobody is coming until dawn.

Split up to keep it running

Generators, pumps, the comms mast, the helipad lights. The jobs are spread across the whole rig, so the crew can't stay together for long.

Don't trust the radio

Your walkie is the only link to the others, and it replays your crew's real voices from earlier in the night. You can't always tell who is talking.

Count heads, then count again

Fall a few steps behind and you'll wake up somewhere else, alone, without your radio. The one walking with your friends now isn't you.

Nobody sees the same thing

Each player's screen can show things the others don't. The only way to almost see it is the old Polaroid, and the photos are never clear.

Install

  1. Download the zip and unzip it anywhere.
  2. Run the game's .exe inside the folder.
  3. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose More info, then Run anyway.

You'll need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • At least one friend (2 to 8 players)
  • A microphone and headphones

About your voice

Voice chat is recorded during a session so the creature can replay it. Recordings stay in that session and aren't kept afterwards.
